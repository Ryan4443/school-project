02 · How It Works (+ A Hint of Science)

Photovoltaic
& Thermal Tech.

As stated in your NCERT Geography textbook, Photovoltaic (PV) technology converts sunlight directly into electricity.
🔬 Just a Hint of Science: Inside each solar panel is a wafer of Silicon (Si)—made from ordinary sand—printed with fine Silver (Ag) grid lines. When sunlight particles (photons) hit the silicon, they nudge electrons into a steady flow of electric current!

1. Photovoltaic (PV) Technology: Converts sunlight directly into electricity using silicon solar cells with zero moving parts, zero smoke, and zero fuel cost.

05 · NCERT Core Topic — Rural Impact & Agriculture

Save Forests
& Farm Manure.

In rural India, over 70% of household energy traditionally came from firewood and cattle dung cakes. NCERT highlights that solar power minimises dependence on firewood and dung cakes—which directly conserves trees and saves valuable dung manure for agriculture!

Interactive Village Solar Grid: Click Capture Sunlight to energize the solar grid, Power Village to send clean electricity to homes and farm pumps, or Compare Firewood Smoke to see heat loss from traditional fuels.
Preserves Organic Manure for Farming: Burning cattle dung cakes destroys rich soil nutrients. Replacing dung cakes with solar cookers and solar electricity allows farmers to use cattle dung as natural organic manure, boosting crop yields!
