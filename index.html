Climate & Soil Requirements

CBSE Class 10 Social Science • Agriculture

🌡️ Temperature

Thrives in a hot and humid tropical/subtropical climate with temperature between 21°C to 27°C. Severe frost arrests stem elongation.

★ Exam Point: Frost damages sucrose yield

🌧️ Rainfall & Moisture

Annual rainfall of 75 cm to 100 cm. In dry tracts of UP, Haryana, and Maharashtra, extensive canal and drip irrigation is essential.

★ Exam Point: Irrigation required in dry spells

🌱 Soil & Topography

Grows vigorously on deep rich alluvial soils of Indo-Gangetic plains and well-drained black regur soils of Peninsular India.

★ Exam Point: Flat land enables easy transport

👨‍🌾 Manual Labour

Highly labour-intensive crop requiring cheap manual labour for sowing setts, earthing up, harvesting, and rapid mill delivery within 24h.

★ Exam Point: Crushing must occur within 24h

Bihar & Punjab Northern belt producers benefiting from perennial Himalayan river networks. Alluvial plains; extensive canal networks. ★ Northern subtropical growing tract.

North India vs South India

Geographical Shift of the Sugar Industry

Parameter North India (Subtropical) South India (Tropical Peninsular)
Climatic Conditions Extreme summer heat and cold winter frost restrict cane maturity. Equable maritime climate free from frost; ideal year-round growth.
Sucrose Recovery Lower sugar recovery (9% - 10%) due to dry desiccating winds. Higher sugar recovery (11% - 12.5%) due to coastal humidity.
Crushing Season Short crushing season: only 4 months (November to February). Longer crushing season: 7 to 8 months (October to May).
Sugar Mill Structure Predominantly older private mills with outdated machinery. Highly successful cooperative sugar factories owned by farmers.

Industrial Uses & By-Products

Circular Economy in Sugar Refining

🌾 Bagasse (Residual Fiber)

The fibrous pulp remaining after crushing cane stalks. Serves as green biofuel generating steam and power for mills, and raw pulp for paper/cardboard.

🍯 Molasses (Cane Treacle)

Viscous syrup separated during sugar crystallization. Essential raw material for fermentation distilleries producing industrial alcohol, rum, and fuel ethanol.

🧪 Press Mud (Filter Cake)

Mineral and organic sediment filtered from raw cane juice. Rich in nitrogen, phosphorus, and humic acid; recycled as excellent soil conditioner.

🍃 Green Tops & Leaves

Harvested foliage stripped from cane tops in the field, supplying valuable nutritious green fodder for rural dairy livestock.
