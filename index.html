THE CULM & NODES

Saccharum officinarum Structure

The sugarcane stalk (culm) is partitioned into barrel-shaped internodes separated by prominent nodal joints. Each node contains an intercalary growth ring, a dormant vegetative eye bud, and a band of adventitious root primordia.

3.5 – 4.5 m
Phyllotaxy

STEP 04 // LOGISTICS & BIOCHEMISTRY

THE 24-HR RACE

Convoys Against the Invertase Clock

Once severed from the root stool, the cane sugars begin to deplete immediately. Natural invertase enzymes rapidly hydrolyze sucrose into non-crystallizable reducing sugars (glucose and fructose).

Max Delivery Window
24 Hours
Daily Sucrose Loss
0.8% – 1.5%

★ Class 10 Fact: Sugar mills are situated directly inside cultivation belts because transporting heavy, perishable stalks over long distances causes severe economic sugar loss.

STEP 06 // CIRCULAR BIO-ECONOMY

BY-PRODUCTS & POWER

100% Zero-Waste Agriculture

Sugarcane is one of the world's most efficient renewable biomass sources. Nothing is thrown away during commercial processing.

🔥 Bagasse (Crushed Cane Fiber): Burned inside high-pressure cogeneration boilers to generate 100% of the mill's electricity, with surplus power exported to the state electrical grid. Also manufactured into eco-paper.
🧪 Molasses: Thick dark viscous syrup fermented in distilleries into potable spirits and 99.8% anhydrous fuel-grade ethanol for India's 20% national petrol blending target.
🌱 Press Mud (Filter Cake): Calcium and phosphorus rich organic residue utilized as high-potency soil fertilizer in agricultural fields.
