Climate & Soil Requirements

CBSE Class 10 Board Essentials

🌡️ Temperature

Requires a hot and humid climate with temperatures between 21°C to 27°C. Severe frost arrests growth, while excessive heat during ripening reduces juice content.

★ Board Point: Frost is fatal to the crop

🌧️ Annual Rainfall

Needs an annual rainfall of 75 cm to 100 cm. In regions of low rainfall, intensive canal or tubewell irrigation is compulsory.

★ Board Point: Irrigation essential in dry periods

🌱 Soil & Terrain

Can grow on a variety of soils: deep rich alluvial soils, black soils, and loams with high moisture-retention capacity and proper drainage.

★ Board Point: Flat plains facilitate irrigation

👨‍🌾 Labour Requirement

Highly labour-intensive crop requiring cheap manual labour from planting, hoeing, and weeding to cutting and harvesting.

★ Board Point: Stalks must be crushed within 24h

North India vs South India

Why the Sugar Industry Shifted to the South

Factor North India (Subtropical) Peninsular South (Tropical)
Climate Extreme summer heat & severe winter frost hamper cane yield. Maritime tropical climate free from frost; ideal for vegetative growth.
Sucrose Content Lower juice and sugar content due to dry winds during ripening. Significantly higher sucrose concentration due to sustained coastal humidity.
Crushing Season Shorter crushing window (typically November to February: 4 months). Much longer crushing season (October to May: 7-8 months), keeping mills profitable.
Mill Ownership Dominated by private mills with outdated processing machinery. Well-organized cooperative sugar mills with modern, energy-efficient equipment.

Industrial Uses & By-Products

Circular Bio-Economy in Sugar Mills

🌾 Bagasse (Crushed Fiber)

The dry pulpy fibrous residue left after extracting juice. Used as clean biofuel to power mill boilers and raw material for eco-paper and cardboard manufacturing.

🍯 Molasses (Dark Syrup)

Thick, dark syrup separated during sugar crystallization. Crucial feedstock for distilleries producing industrial alcohol, rum, and green ethanol for fuel blending.

🧪 Press Mud (Filter Cake)

The residue from juice filtration. Rich in organic matter, nitrogen, phosphorus, and calcium; widely utilized as natural organic fertilizer for farms.

🍃 Green Cane Tops

Nutritious green crown foliage collected during field harvest, providing essential cattle fodder for rural livestock.
