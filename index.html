ACT 06 // INDUSTRIAL MIGRATION

The camera pans up to an isometric 2.5D topographical relief plate of India. Coastal breeze waves sweep across Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu, illustrating why the sugar industry relocated from the chilly northern plains to the peninsular south.

Peninsular South (Leader)
  • Maritime tropical climate prevents winter frost
  • Higher sucrose content & juice recovery (11–13%)
  • Extended crushing season: 7 to 8 months
  • Thriving farmer-owned cooperative mills
Northern Plains (UP/Bihar)
  • Severe winter frost triggers sucrose inversion
  • Shorter crushing season: only 4 to 5 months
  • Lower recovery rate (9–10%)
  • Older, smaller private proprietary mills
Key Producers: Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Bihar, Punjab, and Haryana.
