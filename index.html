CHAPTER 01 // 3D MORPH: THE SPROUTING SETT

THE STEM SETT

Farmer Planting Technology & Bud Germination

Sugarcane is never grown from seeds. Indian farmers plant stem setts (गन्ने के टुकड़े) with 2–3 swollen nodal buds directly into moist soil furrows. In the 3D viewport, watch an actual cutting anchored in rich alluvial earth with a fresh green tiller shoot pushing upwards and delicate white rootlets spreading into the soil.

Botanical Unit
Stem Setts
Setts Per Hectare
~35,000 Setts
Germination Window
12 – 18 Days
Eye-Bud Alignment
180° Alternating
CBSE Board Focus: Vegetative propagation via setts guarantees genetically identical clones with uniform sucrose recovery and protects young tillers from seed blight.

CHAPTER 04 // 3D MORPH: INDIA'S SUGAR BELT MAP

THE PENINSULAR SHIFT

Why Sugar Mills Migrated to South India

A 3D topographical map of India emerges in the viewport. Glowing amber markers highlight the traditional Northern plains (UP & Bihar), while radiant emerald beacons and tropical maritime breeze streams highlight Peninsular India (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and Telangana).

Peninsular South (Leader)
  • Higher Sucrose: Marine tropical humidity prevents drying up of cane juice.
  • Longer Crushing: 7 to 8 months without winter frost interruptions.
  • Cooperative Success: Flourishing farmer-run cooperative mills.
Northern Plains (Traditional)
  • Shorter Season: Only 3 to 4 winter months before summer heat.
  • Frost Risk: Severe frost destroys sucrose via inversion.
  • Older Machinery: Traditional private mills with higher transit delay.
CBSE 5-Mark Must-Know: Four decisive factors: 1) Maritime climate preventing drying up, 2) Higher sucrose yield per quintal, 3) Longer crushing season of 7–8 months, and 4) Outstanding efficiency of cooperative mills in Maharashtra.

CHAPTER 06 // 3D MORPH: BOILING GUR & E20 NOZZLE

CIRCULAR BIO-ECONOMY

Gur, Khandsari, Bagasse Electricity & E20 Ethanol

The 3D model transforms into a traditional iron boiling kadhai filled with bubbling golden **Gur (Jaggery / गुड़)** and solid jaggery blocks, alongside a modern **E20 Bio-Ethanol Fuel Nozzle** with a clean bio-flame and green electrical sparks from burning bagasse!

⚡

Bagasse (Crushed Fiber)

Burned in cogeneration boilers to generate 100% of mill electricity, exporting surplus green power to the state electrical grid. Also manufactured into eco-paper.

⛽

Molasses & Ethanol (E20)

Dark viscous residue fermented into anhydrous 99.8% bio-ethanol for India's 20% petrol blending mandate, cutting crude imports and vehicular greenhouse gases.

🌱

Press Mud (Filter Cake)

Mineral and phosphorus-rich organic residue recycled directly back to farmer fields as a potent natural organic soil conditioner.
