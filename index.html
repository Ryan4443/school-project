04 • HARVEST SHEARING

TRANS-SECTION CUT

Clean base cuts sever the stalk at peak sucrose saturation. The 3D view angles directly into the circular cross-section, revealing the tough outer rind, concentric vascular fibers, and the dense sucrose core.

THE 24-HOUR BIOLOGICAL COUNTDOWN

Once cut, natural invertase enzymes immediately begin hydrolyzing sucrose into reducing glucose and fructose. Convoys of tractors race against the clock to reach mills within 24 hours.

07 • CIRCULAR BIO-ENERGY

ZERO-WASTE LOOP

Sugarcane is 100% utilized in a closed-loop green industrial cycle:

1. BAGASSE: Cogeneration fuel for self-powered steam & electricity + paper.
2. MOLASSES: Distilled into potable spirits and 99.8% fuel ethanol (E20 national target).
3. PRESS MUD: Calcium-rich organic field fertilizer returning soil nutrients.
